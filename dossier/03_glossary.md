# ICT Glossary

## BPR

- Meaning: `Balanced Price Range`
- Status: `community-extension`
- Short definition: overlap between opposing FVG zones.
- Do not confuse with: a standard single-direction FVG.

## BOS

- Meaning: `Break of Structure`
- Status: `community-standard`
- Short definition: a structure break used to show continuation or trend development.
- Do not confuse with: MSS, which is often used more specifically for directional shift.

## BSL

- Meaning: `Buy-Side Liquidity`
- Status: `community-standard`
- Short definition: liquidity resting above highs.
- Do not confuse with: a directional bias by itself.

## CE

- Meaning: `Consequent Encroachment`
- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: midpoint of a fair value gap.
- Do not confuse with: the full width of the FVG.

## DRT

- Meaning: `Determine Dealing Range`
- Status: `house-canonical`
- Short definition: the process of selecting the active dealing range before judging liquidity, bias, narrative, or execution.
- Do not confuse with: simply drawing two arbitrary swing points without a liquidity or narrative reason.

## Dealing Range

- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: the swing range used to define premium, discount, equilibrium, and the internal/external liquidity map.
- Do not confuse with: a random zoom window on the chart.

## Displacement

- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: forceful repricing that often leaves inefficiency behind.
- Do not confuse with: any ordinary impulse candle.

## BISI

- Meaning: `Buy-Side Imbalance / Sell-Side Inefficiency`
- Status: `community-standard`
- Short definition: a bullish fair value gap read as a supportive imbalance inside the dealing range.
- Do not confuse with: every bullish candle gap without location, narrative, or displacement.

## Equilibrium

- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: the midpoint of the dealing range.
- Do not confuse with: CE, which is the midpoint of an FVG.

## ERL

- Meaning: `External Range Liquidity`
- Status: `community-standard`
- Short definition: liquidity outside or at the extremes of the dealing range.
- Do not confuse with: internal range liquidity inside the range.

## FVG

- Meaning: `Fair Value Gap`
- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: a three-candle imbalance where the first and third candles do not fully overlap, usually created by displacement and then judged by where it forms in the dealing range.
- Do not confuse with: any random gap between candles that lacks context, location, or follow-through.

## IFVG

- Meaning: `Inversion Fair Value Gap`
- Status: `community-extension`
- Short definition: a failed FVG that is later treated as a flipped zone after price clearly disrespects the original imbalance and begins using it in the opposite way.
- Do not confuse with: a normal active FVG.

## IRL

- Meaning: `Internal Range Liquidity`
- Status: `community-standard`
- Short definition: liquidity that sits inside the active dealing range.
- Do not confuse with: ERL at the outer edges or beyond the range.

## Judas Swing

- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: a deceptive early-session move that raids liquidity before the intended move develops.
- Do not confuse with: every false breakout.

## Kill Zone

- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: a session window where ICT traders expect important movement or setup formation.
- Do not confuse with: any time of day that happens to be volatile.

## MSS

- Meaning: `Market Structure Shift`
- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: a structure change that suggests directional shift after sweep or displacement.
- Do not confuse with: a small internal break with no context.

## OB

- Meaning: `Order Block`
- Status: `community-standard`
- Short definition: a candle or zone treated as the origin of institutional repricing, but only meaningful after house rules define where it forms in the dealing range and how price respects or disrespects it.
- Do not confuse with: every last opposite candle before a move.

## BB

- Meaning: `Breaker Block`
- Status: `community-extension`
- Short definition: a failed or violated order-block-style area that later acts as a flipped zone in the opposite direction.
- Do not confuse with: any ordinary retest after price moves away.

## MB

- Meaning: `Mitigation Block`
- Status: `community-extension`
- Short definition: a block or candle area revisited to rebalance or mitigate earlier order flow before continuation.
- Do not confuse with: every retracement into a prior candle.

## RB

- Meaning: `Rejection Block`
- Status: `community-extension`
- Short definition: a candle body or small zone whose rejection behavior becomes meaningful when price wicks into it and closes back through it in context.
- Do not confuse with: any single wick without dealing-range or narrative context.

## OTE

- Meaning: `Optimal Trade Entry`
- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: a preferred retracement entry zone used inside a larger narrative.
- Do not confuse with: a complete strategy by itself.

## PD Array

- Meaning: `Premium/Discount Array`
- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: the set of reaction or inefficiency zones used inside a dealing range, including FVG, IFVG, OB, BB, MB, RB, and related arrays.
- Do not confuse with: a single specific pattern.

## Respect / Disrespect

- Status: `house-canonical`
- Short definition: the read on whether price is honoring or violating a PD array through wick behavior, closes, repeated acceptance, or repeated failure beyond the zone.
- Do not confuse with: touching a zone once and assuming it held or failed.

## Power of 3

- Status: `official`
- Short definition: accumulation, manipulation, distribution.
- Do not confuse with: a deterministic sequence that always resolves identically.

## Premium / Discount

- Status: `official/community-standard`
- Short definition: the upper and lower halves of the chosen dealing range.
- Do not confuse with: value judgment without a consistent dealing range.

## SIBI

- Meaning: `Sell-Side Imbalance / Buy-Side Inefficiency`
- Status: `community-standard`
- Short definition: a bearish fair value gap read as a downward imbalance inside the dealing range.
- Do not confuse with: every bearish candle gap without location, narrative, or displacement.

## SSL

- Meaning: `Sell-Side Liquidity`
- Status: `community-standard`
- Short definition: liquidity resting below lows.
- Do not confuse with: a bearish bias by itself.
