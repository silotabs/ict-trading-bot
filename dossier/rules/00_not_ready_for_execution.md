# Execution Guardrail

This research dossier is not yet a live-trading specification.

## Not Ready Yet

The following are still missing:

- your house definitions
- your market and timeframe scope
- your setup filters
- your invalidation rules
- your risk model
- your execution venue and order constraints

## What The Assistant Can Do At This Stage

- summarize ICT concepts
- compare definitions across sources
- annotate charts or TradingView screenshots
- draft rules from your explanations
- build a paper-trading checklist

## What The Assistant Should Not Do At This Stage

- place live trades
- claim a setup is valid without your house rules
- treat ambiguous community labels as strict signals
- infer risk limits that you have not approved
